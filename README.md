Entrants write one single line that makes office life funny: the Monday meeting, the reply-all, the printer that jams at the worst moment. Every line is put against another line by the house judge, two at a time, and the lines that keep winning rise to the top. Five places are paid from the $2 reward and entering costs nothing. The lines that do best turn on a real detail of office life and land the laugh in the last few words.

The entry is one line: **The printer only jams on the one page you need, at 4:59, in front of the boss who asked for it at five.** — 103 characters. One concrete office detail, three specific pressures, and the punch lands on the final beat.
